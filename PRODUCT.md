# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Taite, receiving a personal romantic date invitation from Kaevon via a shareable link on her phone or desktop.

## Product Purpose

A single-page interactive experience that asks Taite on a date in a playful yet sincere way. Success is a confirmed "yes" with calendar details saved and a memorable, premium-feeling moment.

## Brand Personality

Romantic, playful, sincere. Premium and personal without being cheesy. The mangopookie name carries warmth; the rest stays honest and direct.

## Anti-references

Generic AI landing pages (purple gradients, cream backgrounds, Inter font). Cheesy stock romance clichés. Password gates. Music autoplay. Inside jokes beyond the brand name.

## Design Principles

1. Playful on the No dodge, sincere on Yes and confirmation.
2. Motion with purpose: spring physics, reduced-motion fallbacks.
3. Mobile-first: touch dodge, safe areas, large tap targets.
4. One clear path forward: only Yes advances the flow.
5. Premium craft over prototype polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on buttons and body text. prefers-reduced-motion respected. Native dialog for modal. Semantic HTML and keyboard-accessible Yes flow.
