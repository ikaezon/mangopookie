# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Taite, receiving a personal romantic weekend itinerary and date confirmation from Kaevon via a shareable link on her phone (iPhone-first) or desktop.

## Product Purpose

A multi-screen mobile story that walks Taite through a curated weekend itinerary (Welcome → Ready → Friday → Saturday), then asks her to lock the official date. Success is a confirmed "yes" with venue and calendar details saved and a memorable, premium-feeling moment.

## Characters

- Kaevon: boy mango mando
- Taite: sugar plum pookie / mangopookie

## Brand Personality

Romantic, playful, sincere. Pool-summer energy with personal nicknames and polaroid photos. Premium and personal without being cheesy.

## Flow

1. Welcome — nickname marquee + surprise line
2. Ready — "are u ready"
3. Friday — June 12 itinerary (wine, Fortnite, vodka pasta) + pool polaroid
4. Saturday — breakfast + Cassel Earth + night polaroids
5. Date ask — Yes-only CTA (no runaway No)
6. Date modal — venue, map, datetime (VenueSelect, no native select autoplay)
7. Celebration — confirmation + Google Calendar

## Anti-references

Generic AI landing pages (purple gradients, cream backgrounds, Inter font). Cheesy stock romance clichés. Password gates. Music autoplay. Runaway No button on the main path.

## Design Principles

1. Story first, date lock second — itinerary builds anticipation.
2. Motion with purpose: screen transitions, marquee; reduced-motion fallbacks.
3. Mobile-first: Back/Next nav, safe areas, large tap targets, 100dvh layouts.
4. One clear path forward: only Yes advances to the modal.
5. Premium craft over prototype polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on buttons and body text. prefers-reduced-motion respected. Native dialog for modal. Semantic HTML and keyboard-accessible Yes flow.
